A little room to focus

The Olive Journal · September 9, 2026 · Reading notes

A good reading experience begins with room for the words. On the web, a story often arrives surrounded by navigation, promotions, and invitations to go somewhere else. Focus mode gives the story a quieter place to live.

Click Focus in Olive’s toolbar to try it on this page. The surrounding banners and menus disappear, leaving a comfortable column of text. Open Appearance to choose a font size and switch between a warm light page and a dark reading surface.

Make space for the story

Reading is an invitation to stay with one idea for a while. A paragraph sets out a thought, a second paragraph adds detail, and a heading gives us a place to pause. When these parts work together, the page has a rhythm that needs very little decoration.

Focus mode keeps that structure. Emphasis still has weight, a quiet aside still has its own voice, and links remain useful. You can jump to a later section or follow a link to another reading page.

Attention feels easier when the page gives it somewhere to settle.

A page that fits you

There is no single font size that suits every reader or every moment. Increase the text when you want to sit a little farther from the screen. Use smaller text when a compact view feels comfortable. The headings, paragraphs, and code scale together so the page keeps its shape.

  • Choose a reading size from 14 to 32 pixels.
  • Use Light or Dark to change the reading surface.
  • Hide the appearance panel when the page feels right.
  • Press Escape or click Exit Focus to return to the original page.

Your choices stay available while Olive is running. Entering and leaving Focus mode does not reload the document. When you follow a link to another document, the new page opens in the regular browser view.

A small ritual

Before starting a longer article, take a moment to make the page comfortable. Adjust the size, choose the appearance, and let the controls disappear. These are small changes, but they make room for the work of reading: noticing details, connecting ideas, and deciding what deserves a second look.

  1. Find something you want to read.
  2. Give the words a comfortable size.
  3. Stay with one paragraph at a time.

Keep the useful details

Some stories need a picture or a code example. Focus mode keeps article images that Olive has already loaded, and preserves code and selectable text. A document can still speak in more than one language: שלום, café, and hello all have a place here.

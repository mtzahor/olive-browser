Find the olive

Press Cmd+F or Ctrl+F, then search for olive. Matching text is highlighted, including across inline formatting.

The first olive is green. The second OLIVE is black. A third olive grows on the tree.

Try a Unicode search too: café, שלום, or Mediterranean.

Use Enter or Shift+Enter in the find field, F3 or Shift+F3, or Cmd/Ctrl+G to move between matches. Escape closes find before leaving Focus mode.

A few useful shortcuts

Cmd/Ctrl+L selects the address. Cmd/Ctrl+R reloads. Alt+Left and Alt+Right move through history.

Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+H opens saved history. The latest 1,000 addresses are kept on this computer, with titles, visit counts, and last-visited times.
